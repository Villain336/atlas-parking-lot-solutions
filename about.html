Our Story

Built on Quality, Driven by Service

Atlas Parking Lot Solutions was founded with a simple mission: to provide Greensboro and the surrounding Triad area with unmatched parking lot services. What started as a small family operation has grown into one of the region's most trusted names in asphalt paving, sealcoating, and striping.

For more than 20 years, we've served commercial properties, retail centers, industrial facilities, and municipalities throughout Greensboro, Winston-Salem, High Point, and beyond. Our growth has been fueled by word-of-mouth referrals and repeat clients who trust us to deliver exceptional results every time.

We remain committed to the values that built our business: honest pricing, superior craftsmanship, ADA compliance expertise, and the use of premium materials and modern equipment. Every member of our team takes pride in contributing to safer, more attractive properties across the Triad.
