Our Story

Atlas Parking Lot Solutions was founded by Jamaal with a simple mission: to provide the Piedmont Triad region with reliable, professional parking lot services that property owners and managers can truly depend on. As a locally owned and operated business based in Greensboro, NC, we understand the unique needs of North Carolina properties and the importance of maintaining safe, compliant, and visually appealing parking facilities.

What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted name across Greensboro, Winston-Salem, High Point, Burlington, and surrounding communities. We've built our reputation on quality workmanship, honest pricing, and showing up when we say we will. Every stripe we paint, every crack we fill, and every lot we maintain reflects our commitment to excellence and our pride in serving our local community.

Today, Atlas Parking Lot Solutions serves hundreds of commercial properties, retail centers, churches, medical facilities, apartment complexes, and industrial sites throughout the Piedmont Triad.
