Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about parking lot maintenance, striping, and ADA compliance.

How often should parking lot striping be redone?

High-traffic lots need restriping every 12-18 months, while lower-traffic lots can go 18-24 months. Climate, paint quality, and traffic volume all impact longevity. We assess your specific conditions and recommend an optimal maintenance schedule to keep your lot safe and compliant.

What are the ADA requirements for parking lot striping?

ADA requires accessible spaces of at least 96 inches wide with 60-inch access aisles, proper signage at 5 feet above ground, and at least one van-accessible space per six accessible spaces. We ensure full compliance with federal ADA standards and North Carolina state requirements on every project.

How much does parking lot striping cost?

Costs vary based on lot size, complexity, number of spaces, and paint type. We provide free, no-obligation estimates tailored to your specific property needs. Most commercial lots range from $500-$3,000 depending on scope. Contact us today for an accurate quote.

What causes potholes in parking lots?

Potholes form from water seeping into cracks, expanding during freeze-thaw cycles, and weakening the base layer. Heavy traffic accelerates deterioration of compromised areas. Regular sealcoating and crack filling prevent water infiltration and extend pavement life significantly.
