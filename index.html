Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about parking lot maintenance and striping services.

How often should parking lot striping be redone?
High-traffic lots need restriping every 12-18 months, while lower-traffic lots can go 18-24 months. Climate, paint quality, and traffic volume all impact longevity.
What are the ADA requirements for parking lot striping?
ADA requires accessible spaces of at least 96 inches wide with 60-inch access aisles, proper signage at 5 feet above ground, and at least one van-accessible space per six accessible spaces.
How much does parking lot striping cost?
Costs vary based on lot size, complexity, number of spaces, and paint type. We provide free, no-obligation estimates tailored to your specific property needs.
What causes potholes in parking lots?
Potholes form from water seeping into cracks, expanding during freeze-thaw cycles, and weakening the base layer. Heavy traffic accelerates deterioration of compromised areas.
