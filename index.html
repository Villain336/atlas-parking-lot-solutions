Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about parking lot maintenance

How often should parking lot striping be redone?

Parking lot striping should typically be redone every 18-24 months depending on traffic volume and weather conditions. High-traffic areas may require restriping annually, while low-traffic lots can last 2-3 years. Regular maintenance ensures visibility, safety, and ADA compliance. We recommend inspecting your striping twice a year and scheduling maintenance when lines become faded or difficult to see.

What are the ADA requirements for parking lot striping?

ADA requirements include: accessible parking spaces must be at least 96 inches wide with a 60-inch access aisle, van-accessible spaces need 96-inch access aisles, proper signage with the International Symbol of Accessibility, and compliant slope ratios. The number of required accessible spaces depends on total lot capacity. Our team stays current with all ADA regulations and ensures your parking lot meets or exceeds these standards.

How much does parking lot striping cost?

Parking lot striping costs typically range from $0.15 to $0.30 per linear foot, depending on project size, paint type, and complexity. A standard parking space costs $25-$75 to stripe. Factors affecting price include surface condition, number of spaces, custom markings, and ADA compliance requirements. Contact us for a free, detailed estimate tailored to your specific project.

What causes potholes in parking lots?

Potholes form when water seeps into pavement cracks, freezes and expands, then thaws. This freeze-thaw cycle weakens the asphalt base. Heavy traffic over weakened areas causes the surface to collapse, creating potholes. Poor drainage, aged pavement, and inadequate base preparation also contribute to pothole formation. Regular sealcoating and crack filling can prevent pothole development.
