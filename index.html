FAQ

Quick answers that help property managers and owners make decisions faster.

How much does parking lot striping cost in North Carolina?

Pricing depends on lot size, layout complexity, paint type, number of stalls, ADA requirements, and whether restriping follows existing lines. The fastest way to get an accurate price is a quick site walkthrough or photos + measurements.

How long does striping paint take to dry?

Dry time varies by product and weather. Many lots can reopen the same day, but cure time is influenced by temperature, humidity, and surface condition.

Can you add ADA-compliant parking and access aisles?

Yes. We can lay out handicap/van-accessible stalls, access aisles, and related markings so your lot is clearly marked and easier to navigate.

Do you offer crack filling before restriping?

Yes. If cracking is present, crack filling helps slow water intrusion and improves the finished appearance before restriping.

What businesses do you typically serve?

We commonly work with retail centers, offices, churches, medical sites, warehouses, apartments/HOAs, and mixed-use properties.
