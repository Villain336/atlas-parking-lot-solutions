ADA Compliance Solutions

The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) requires all commercial parking lots to provide accessible parking spaces that meet specific federal standards. Non-compliance can result in fines ranging from $75,000 to $150,000 for first violations, along with costly lawsuits and damage to your reputation. Our ADA compliance service ensures your parking lot meets all federal accessibility requirements, protecting you from legal liability while serving all customers with dignity and respect.

We conduct thorough assessments of your existing spaces, identify deficiencies, and implement complete solutionsâfrom proper dimensions and slope requirements to signage height and color specifications. Let us help you create a welcoming, compliant environment for everyone.
