Pothole Filling & Asphalt Repair

Potholes are more than just an eyesoreâthey're a liability that can damage vehicles, cause injuries, and create a poor impression of your property. Our professional pothole filling and asphalt repair services restore the integrity and safety of your parking lot using high-quality materials and proven techniques that deliver long-lasting results.

We understand that every pothole is different, which is why we assess each repair individually to determine the best approach. Whether you have a single pothole or multiple areas of deterioration, our experienced team will restore your pavement to optimal condition.

Our Pothole Repair Process

1

Assessment

We thoroughly inspect the damaged area to determine the extent of deterioration, identify underlying causes, and recommend the most effective repair method.

2

Preparation

The pothole is cleaned of all loose debris, vegetation, and water. We remove unstable material and create clean edges to ensure proper adhesion of repair materials.

3

Repair

We apply high-quality hot-mix asphalt or cold-patch material depending on conditions and requirements. The material is carefully placed and compacted to match surrounding pavement.

4

Finishing

The repaired area is compacted to proper density, smoothed to match surrounding grade, and sealed if necessary to prevent water infiltration and extend the life of the repair.

Benefits of Professional Pothole Repair

  • Liability Protection: Reduce exposure to injury claims and lawsuits
  • Improved Appearance: Maintain professional property aesthetics
  • Prevent Further Damage: Stop small problems from becoming expensive failures
  • Extended Pavement Life: Protect your asphalt investment with timely repairs
  • Cost-Effective: Early repair costs far less than full pavement replacement

ADA Compliance Solutions

The Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) establishes specific requirements for accessible parking spaces and facilities. Non-compliance can result in significant fines, lawsuits, and damage to your reputation. Our ADA compliance services ensure your parking lot meets all federal accessibility standards while creating a welcoming environment for all visitors.

We stay current with ADA regulations and best practices to deliver compliant solutions that protect your business and serve your community. From initial assessment to final installation, we handle every aspect of bringing your parking facility into full compliance.

What Our ADA Compliance Services Cover

Accessible Space Dimensions

We ensure parking spaces meet the required 96-inch minimum width with proper access aisles, calculated based on your total parking capacity and facility type.

Van-Accessible Spaces

Installation of wider van-accessible spaces (132 inches including aisle) with proper signage indicating van accessibility as required by ADA standards.

Signage Installation

Mounting of International Symbol of Accessibility signs at required height (60 inches minimum to sign bottom) with proper visibility and van-accessible designation where needed.

Access Aisles

Properly marked access aisles adjacent to accessible spaces, with required 60-inch minimum width and distinctive diagonal striping to prevent parking in these critical areas.

Slope Requirements

Assessment and correction of slope issues to ensure accessible spaces and access routes meet maximum 2% grade requirements in all directions.

Why ADA Compliance Matters

Legal Protection: Avoid costly fines, lawsuits, and legal complications by meeting federal requirements from the start.

Community Access: Ensure individuals with disabilities can safely and independently access your facility, demonstrating corporate responsibility.

Business Reputation: Show that your organization values inclusivity and accessibility for all customers and visitors.

Risk Management: Proactive compliance prevents expensive retrofits and potential litigation down the road.

Increased Patronage: Accessible facilities welcome a broader customer base and improve overall visitor experience.
