How long does it take for parking lot paint to dry?

Drying time depends on weather conditions, temperature, humidity, and paint type. In favorable Greensboro weather conditions (warm and dry), water-based traffic paint typically dries to touch in 15-30 minutes and is ready for light traffic in 1-2 hours. Full cure takes 24-48 hours. We schedule projects during optimal weather windows and can work during off-hours to minimize disruption to your business operations.

What are ADA requirements for handicap parking spaces?

ADA-compliant handicap parking requires specific dimensions, access aisles, proper signage, and clear markings. Standard accessible spaces need 8-foot-wide stalls with 5-foot access aisles, while van-accessible spaces require 11-foot-wide stalls or 8-foot stalls with 8-foot aisles. The number of required accessible spaces is based on total parking capacity. Atlas Parking Lot Solutions ensures your Greensboro property meets all federal ADA requirements to avoid compliance violations.

How often should I sealcoat my parking lot in Greensboro?

In Greensboro's climate with hot summers and variable winters, parking lots should be sealcoated every 2-3 years to protect against weather damage, UV rays, and traffic wear. New asphalt should cure for 6-12 months before the first sealcoat application. High-traffic areas may benefit from more frequent application. Regular sealcoating extends pavement life significantly and maintains a professional appearance while reducing long-term maintenance and repair costs.

Can you repair potholes and cracks before restriping?

Yes, Atlas Parking Lot Solutions offers complete parking lot restoration in Greensboro. We strongly recommend filling potholes and sealing cracks before restriping to ensure a smooth, professional finish and prevent stripe paint from settling into depressions. Our comprehensive approach addresses underlying pavement issues first, then applies fresh striping for optimal results and longer-lasting markings. This integrated service saves money compared to separate contractor visits.
