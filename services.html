Our Process

Damage Assessment

We inspect the full extent of the damage, measuring depth and checking for base failure or water damage beneath the surface.

Surface Preparation

All loose material, debris, and vegetation is removed. Edges are cut cleanly and the base is inspected and repaired if necessary.

Material Application

Hot-mix asphalt is applied to proper depth. Tack coat ensures strong bonding with existing pavement for a seamless repair.

Compaction & Finishing

Material is compacted with vibratory rollers to meet density specifications, creating a smooth, durable surface that matches surrounding pavement.

Service 03

ADA Compliance Solutions

The Americans with Disabilities Act sets strict requirements for accessible parking, and non-compliance can result in fines ranging from $75,000 to $150,000 for first violations. Beyond the financial risk, providing accessible parking is a legal obligation and a matter of basic fairness.

Our ADA compliance service ensures your parking lot meets all federal accessibility standards. We handle space dimensions, van-accessible spaces, proper signage height and placement, access aisle width, slope requirements, and the distinctive blue paint and International Symbol of Access. We stay current with code changes so you don't have to worry about compliance.

What We Cover

Accessible Space Dimensions

Minimum 96" wide spaces properly marked and dimensioned

Van-Accessible Spaces

Minimum 132" wide spaces with proper van-accessible signage

Signage Requirements

Reflective signs mounted at 60" minimum height from ground to bottom of sign

Access Aisles

Minimum 60" wide access aisles connecting to accessible routes

Slope Requirements

Maximum 2% slope in all directions within spaces and aisles

Blue Paint & Symbol Standards

Proper blue paint color and International Symbol of Access placement

  • Avoids costly ADA violation fines and legal action
  • Ensures equal access for customers and employees
  • Demonstrates corporate social responsibility
  • Meets requirements for business licenses and permits
  • Protects property value and insurability
  • Creates a welcoming environment for all visitors

Additional Services

Comprehensive parking lot maintenance solutions to protect your pavement investment

Sealcoating

Protect your asphalt from sun, water, and chemical damage with professional sealcoating. This cost-effective treatment doubles pavement life, restores rich black color, and provides a smooth, attractive finish. We use commercial-grade sealer applied in two coats for maximum protection and durability.

Crack Sealing

Prevent water infiltration and pavement failure with professional crack sealing. We route and clean cracks, then fill them with hot rubberized sealant that flexes with temperature changes. This preventive maintenance stops small cracks from becoming expensive potholes and extends pavement life by years.

Asphalt Overlay

Restore severely worn parking lots without the expense of full replacement. Our asphalt overlay service adds a new 1.5" to 2" layer of hot-mix asphalt over your existing pavement, creating a fresh surface at a fraction of the cost. Ideal for lots with surface wear but sound structural base.
